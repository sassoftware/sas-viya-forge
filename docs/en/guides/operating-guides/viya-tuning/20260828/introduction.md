Performance and stability are critical to the success of any SAS Viya deployment. As user workloads evolve, users are onboarded, and new software releases are introduced, administrators must understand how the platform is behaving and where potential bottlenecks may exist. The goal of this guide is to help administrators evaluate platform health, understand key performance indicators, and identify areas that may require further investigation or optimization. By establishing a regular process for monitoring and reviewing the environment, organizations can improve overall platform stability, support future growth, and help ensure users continue to have a positive experience with SAS Viya.
